🚀 I completed a Retail Sales Analytics Project where I used Excel to clean raw data (removing duplicates, handling nulls, and fixing errors) and then built an interactive dashboard in Power BI to uncover business insights.
🔧 The workflow included:
•	Data Cleaning in Excel to ensure accuracy and reliability.
•	Power BI for building dynamic dashboards and visualizations.
•	DAX measures to calculate KPIs and support deeper analysis.
•	Bookmarks to create interactive views and enhance storytelling in the dashboard.
🎯 The project was both training and practical, aimed at sharpening my skills in data analysis, visualization, and business intelligence.
📊 Key insights discovered:
•	Small outlet sizes generated higher total sales compared to medium and large outlets.
•	Low Fat products outperformed Regular ones in overall sales.
•	Tier 3 locations contributed the largest share of sales across outlets.
•	Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods were the top-performing product categories.
•	Outlets established in 2018 showed a noticeable sales peak compared to other years.
💡 This project demonstrates how data analytics and business intelligence tools can transform raw numbers into actionable strategies, helping businesses identify profitable products, evaluate outlet efficiency, and improve customer experience.
